# Market context and proposed differentiation

Reviewed 25 October 2026; verification-access follow-up checked 26 October 2026. These are descriptions of published vendor offers. Vendor adoption claims are not independently audited. The comparison does not establish that no equivalent feature exists elsewhere.

| Product | Published offer | Deal Passport's intended emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| [RealOpen](https://realopen.com/get-started) | Crypto-wallet control verification and proof-of-funds letters. Its described letter shows a verified dollar balance. | Prove an issuer-certified threshold while hiding the exact balance. The buyer proof now works on Stellar Testnet with synthetic issuance. |
| [IntegraLayer](https://integralayer.com/) | Property Asset Passport with external data and attestors such as lawyers, valuers and inspectors. | Apply a specific property-authority claim to qualification for one deal. Synthetic seller attestation now works in the Testnet MVP; real evidence issuance remains a pilot requirement. |
| [MERKAO](https://www.merkao.io/) | Identity/proof-of-funds checks, verified counterparties, NDA protection and confidential data rooms. It reports 250+ verified professionals. | Make the private financial threshold and mutual deal policy independently verifiable on Stellar, then require mutual disclosure consent. The synthetic mutual-disclosure workflow now works in the Testnet MVP. |

**Demand inference:** existing specialized services and MERKAO's reported professional network are signals of demand for verification and confidential property workflows. They do not establish high demand for Deal Passport, its conversion rate, revenue or willingness to pay.

**Novelty claim:** the combination of an issuer-bound private buyer proof, a property-specific seller claim and consent-controlled disclosure under the same deal policy. Some competitors already combine counterparty vetting and data rooms. Deal Passport must demonstrate the private proof and enforceable policy difference, rather than claiming that two-sided vetting or property passports are new inventions.

**Pricing hypothesis selected by Oleksiy:** €89 per month **or** per-verification fees, as alternative commercial models. The per-verification amount and demand remain to validate. No paying customers, broker pilot or revenue are claimed.

The current buyer proof uses a synthetic issuer and a single-party demo-only Groth16 setup. Production issuer relationships, real property evidence and security review remain necessary before making production product claims. The seller/disclosure workflow is implemented for synthetic data.

## Free verification access follow-up

The reviewed property/passport competitors did not establish a free embeddable production service for Portuguese seller marketing authority. RealOpen explicitly offers a free buyer proof-of-funds letter, which addresses a different claim. Free identity checks and free consultation of a seller-supplied valid Portuguese registry certificate can support a human-reviewed pilot. Details, costs, source links and an engineering estimate are in [real verification options](REAL_PROPERTY_VERIFICATION_OPTIONS.md).
